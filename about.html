个人简介

吴海轩（Harlen），2024 年毕业于长沙大学，早期深耕新媒体内容运营，熟悉流量逻辑、内容生态与用户增长体系。毕业后进入跨境电商赛道，独立创业，深耕 B 端外贸、海外社媒运营与跨境流量变现，积累了扎实的商业落地与项目操盘经验。

创业期间因身体原因休整半年，跳出传统流量与贸易固化思维，沉浸式研究人工智能落地体系。从 AI 提示词工程、自动化流程搭建、AI 视频矩阵、企业 AI 赋能，到 AI 项目团队管理、商业 SOP 标准化，完成从「传统运营创业者」到「AI 商业落地专家」的职业跃迁。

现阶段专注：企业 AI 自动化落地、业务流程 AI 改造、跨境数字化升级、AI 项目交付与团队赋能，坚持「不做空谈技术，只做业务落地」的职业理念，帮助传统行业用 AI 降本增效、实现数字化迭代。

Harlen Wu, graduated from Changsha University in 2024. He started his career in new media operation and quickly mastered traffic growth and content operation systems. After graduation, he started his cross-border e-commerce business and accumulated rich experience in overseas social media operation, B2B trade and commercial project delivery.

Due to physical health reasons, he took a half-year break during his entrepreneurial period. During this period, he deeply studied artificial intelligence application systems. He completed a career transformation from a traditional operation entrepreneur to a professional AI business implementation expert.

Currently focusing on enterprise AI automation transformation, business process optimization, cross-border digital upgrading and AI team empowerment. He adheres to practical landing logic and helps enterprises reduce costs and improve efficiency through AI technology.
